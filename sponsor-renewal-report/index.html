Sponsors rarely leave because someone measured the deal and it failed. They leave in a cost review, after a takeover or a change of marketing director, when there's no evidence on the table. A Sponsor Outcome Report puts that evidence on the table before the renewal meeting.

What's in the report

  1. One-page verdict. Renewal health score, ROI range with its grade, the three strongest results and a clear recommendation.
  2. Objectives vs results. Every agreed KPI against its target, with attainment and evidence grade.
  3. Delivery audit. What was contracted against what was delivered, asset by asset, with gaps flagged.
  4. Commercial outcomes. Leads, codes, sales and customers, with cost per outcome and attributed revenue.
  5. Next-year package. What to keep, cut and add, and the price logic for the renewal.
  6. Measurement plan. Codes, links, CRM tags and a control region, so next season can reach grade A.

You get a 10 to 15 page PDF, an editable deck for the renewal pitch and a 45-minute review call. See the sample report (fictional club and sponsor).

How it works: ten working days

  1. Day 0, scoping call (30 minutes). Pick one sponsor whose renewal is three to six months away and agree what counts as a result.
  2. Day 1, send your exports. Contract and delivery logs, promo-code reports, CRM lead totals, sales or account totals, any exposure reports. Totals only, nothing personal.
  3. Days 2 to 9, we grade and write. Fixed formulas, every number graded, every assumption stated and checked by hand.
  4. Day 10, review and handover (45 minutes). A walkthrough call, then the PDF and deck, ready for the renewal meeting.

Questions we get asked

When should we start a renewal report?

Three to six months before the renewal meeting. That leaves time to gather the data, review the report and build the next-year package into your pitch.

What if our sponsor won't share their sales data?

Many won't share everything, and that's fine. Promo-code reports, tagged leads and delivery logs often come from your side. We grade what's available and show the sponsor what one more data source would add.

Is this the same as a sponsorship recap report?

It replaces one. A recap shows what was delivered and seen. An outcome report adds what it sold, how strong the evidence is, and what next season should look like.

Do you work with non-league and lower-league clubs?

Yes. The founding partner report is priced for clubs where one sponsor is a large share of commercial income, which is exactly where losing one hurts most.
