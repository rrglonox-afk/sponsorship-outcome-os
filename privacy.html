4. Business-to-business outreach

We may email or message people at sports organisations and brands about Rightsworth when their role makes our services relevant. Every message says who we are and how to opt out. If you reply "no thanks" or ask us to stop, we will add you to a suppression list and not contact you again. You can object at any time by emailing raymond@rightsworth.com.

5. Who we share data with

We use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf under contract:

  • Netlify, Inc.: website and application hosting.
  • IONOS: domain and business email.
  • Google: web fonts are loaded from Google's servers, which receive your IP address when you load a page.

We may also share data with professional advisers (accountants, lawyers) or where the law requires it. We do not sell personal data.

6. International transfers

Some providers process data outside the UK (for example in the United States or the EEA). Where they do, we rely on the UK's adequacy regulations, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, as applicable.

7. How long we keep data

  • Enquiries and prospect contact details: up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you become a client or ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Suppression list (people who asked not to be contacted): kept so we can honour the request.
  • Client records and invoices: 6 years after the end of the financial year, as required for tax.
  • Commercial data shared for a report: deleted within 90 days of delivering the final report unless the client asks us to keep it for a follow-up report.
  • Hosting logs: as set by our hosting provider, normally no more than 30 days.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its use (including objecting to direct marketing at any time), and to data portability. To use any of these rights, email raymond@rightsworth.com. We will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), but please contact us first so we can try to put it right.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or social-media tracking. Our hosting provider may use strictly necessary technical measures to deliver the site securely; these do not require consent. If we ever add analytics or other non-essential cookies, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

Our separate client workspace (used only by our team) stores data in your browser so it can work, and encrypts it before it is saved to our servers.

10. Security

We use HTTPS throughout, keep client workspaces behind login with data encrypted before storage, and limit access to people who need it.
