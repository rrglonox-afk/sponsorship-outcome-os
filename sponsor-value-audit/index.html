Sponsor Value Audit: sponsorship evaluation for brands

Defend the budget, or cut it, with evidence your finance team will sign off. One partnership, audited from your own data, independent of the rights holder.

Book a 15-minute callGrade your evidence in 30s

Sponsorship is one of the first lines a CFO questions in a cost review, because most of the evidence behind it is exposure: impressions, reach and media value. The Sponsor Value Audit evaluates one partnership from your own CRM and sales data, so the answer to "what did we get for it?" is in pounds, with the strength of the evidence stated.

What the audit answers

  • What did this partnership produce in leads, sales and new customers?
  • How strong is each piece of evidence, from grade A (incremental) to grade D (exposure only)?
  • What is the ROI range on the full cost, including activation?
  • Was everything in the contract actually delivered?
  • Should we keep, cut or renegotiate before the contract rolls, and on what terms?

Independent of the rights holder

The recap deck you get from the club, league or event is written by the party that wants you to renew. Our audit works from your data and our fixed formulas. Media value is reported as context and never counted as return, and ROI is always shown as a conservative and a base-case range.

What we need from you

  • The contract or schedule of rights, and any delivery reports from the rights holder
  • Promo-code or tracked-link reports for the partnership
  • CRM lead or account totals tagged to the partnership (or by region and period, if nothing was tagged)
  • Sales totals for the partnership period and a comparison period or region

Totals only. No personal or customer-level data changes hands, and we never train AI models on client data.

Questions we get asked

How is this different from an exposure or media value report?

Exposure providers measure who saw the logo, and do it well. We use their numbers as grade D context, then connect the partnership to your leads, codes, sales and customers.

We didn't set up tracking. Is an audit still worth it?

Usually. Regional and period comparisons can still show grade A or C outcomes, and the audit gives you a measurement plan to write into the renewal so the next decision is easier.

Will the rights holder see the audit?

Only if you choose to share it. Many brands share the measurement plan and keep the commercial detail for their own negotiation.

How long does it take?

About ten working days from receiving the data, including a review call.
